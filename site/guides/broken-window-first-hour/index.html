A broken window: the first hour

What to do before anyone arrives, and what not to do.

Guide492 words

Make it safe first, tidy second

Broken glass in an opening is a hazard before it is a repair. The priority is to stop anyone walking into the opening or handling loose pieces: keep people and pets out of the room, close internal doors, and do not try to remove a panel that is still held at the top.

Loose shards in a frame can drop without warning when a door is shut or a window is moved. If the glass is in a door or a low window, tape a temporary barrier across the opening rather than leaning anything against the glass itself.

Temporary cover, done properly

Cardboard or ply screwed or taped to the frame is the usual temporary measure. Tape it to the frame, not to broken glass. If the opening is exposed to weather, tape the top edge first so water runs over the cover rather than behind it.

Do not force a sheet of anything into the opening to wedge it — a panel under load can crack the remaining glass or damage the frame, which turns a glass replacement into a frame repair.

What to tell us when you call

The suburb, whether the opening is a door, window or shopfront, roughly how big it is, and whether it is currently open to the weather or to the street. A photo of the whole opening — not just the break — is worth more than a description.

Also say what the glass was doing: a bedroom window, a shopfront the public walks past, a balustrade. Those are different urgency levels and sometimes different glass.

Why the replacement may not be the same glass

The pane that broke was the glass that was there, which is not always the glass the location calls for. Where the opening is in a door, alongside one, or low enough to fall into, the replacement has to suit that location rather than match the old pane.

That is also why a maker cannot always promise same-day: common sizes in common make-ups can be quick, but a toughened or laminated panel made to a specific opening has to be manufactured.

In short
  • Keep people out of the room before anything else
  • Tape temporary cover to the frame, never to broken glass
  • Photograph the whole opening, not just the break
  • The right replacement is decided by the location, not by what was there

Can you come out at night?
We are not an emergency 24-hour service and we will not pretend to be. If an opening is unsafe or exposed, say so on the phone and we will tell you honestly what is possible and when.
Will my insurance cover it?
Often, for accidental damage — but that is a question for your insurer. What we can do is provide the written quote and the glass specification your claim needs.
Can you replace just one panel of a double-glazed unit?
Usually the sealed unit is replaced as a unit rather than one pane, because the seal is the part that has failed. We measure the unit and quote on that basis.
