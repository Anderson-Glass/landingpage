Why we publish this.

Most glazing questions have an answer that does not depend on who you hire: why glass is measured after tiling, why a toughened panel cannot be drilled, why the same window in two rooms can need different glass. Knowing those things makes you harder to sell to, which is the point.

What we will not do is put a number on a job we have not seen. Glass is made to the opening, and the make-up depends on the location — so the guides explain the decision without pretending to make it for you.
