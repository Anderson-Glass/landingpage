glazing.

Home base. Narre Warren, Berwick, Cranbourne, Pakenham, Dandenong and the Casey corridor — same-week site visits are normal here.

What we do in South.

Commercial glazing, office partitions, shower screens, glass replacement and mirrors — supplied and installed. Site visits are scheduled by corridor, so the drive is part of the plan rather than an extra.

This is home. The workshop is in Narre Warren East, so the Casey corridor is where a site visit is easiest to arrange and where a second trip — for a re-measure after tiling, or a missing bracket — costs an hour rather than a day.

The housing stock here is mostly 1980s to 2000s brick veneer, which brings its own patterns. Bathrooms from that period are often out of square by more than the tiles suggest, and original windows are commonly single-glazed aluminium with frames that have moved. Later estates bring larger openings and more full-height glass, which changes what the glass has to do structurally.

Commercial work runs along the Princes Highway corridor and through the Dandenong industrial estates: shopfronts, office fitouts inside warehouses, and glazed entries where the door is used by the public a hundred times a day.
