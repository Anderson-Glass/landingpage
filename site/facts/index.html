What we do not do

We do not publish price lists, because a compliant glass make-up depends on the measured opening. We do not sell ready-made screens or flat-pack. We do not run a call centre — the number goes to the person who will measure and install the job.

Quoting

Measure and quote is free across Melbourne metro. The quote names the application, the make-up, the thickness, the hardware and the inclusions, and states the lead time at the time of quoting rather than as a website promise.

Records

Glazing certificates are issued where the job requires one: construction work, builder-specified work, and safety glass installations. The certificate records the glass supplied and the standard it was selected under.
