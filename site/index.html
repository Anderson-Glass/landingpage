Why it matters

Measured once.
Cut once.

Glass cannot be adjusted on site. That single fact decides how a glazing job goes — so the person who quotes also measures, and the measurements are the ones the glass is cut to.

  • Site measure after finishes, not from drawings
  • Glass make-up and thickness named in writing
  • Compliance checked against AS 1288 for the actual location
  • One glazier accountable from quote to final silicone

Standards

Glass is selected,
not guessed.

The same window can need different glass depending on how low it sits, whether it is a door, what is above it and how big the panel is. That is what AS 1288 exists for — and it is why a quote names a make-up rather than a "type of glass".

ApplicationTypical make-upThicknessSelected under
Shopfront, framedToughened monolithic6–10mmAS 1288 / AS 2208
Glass entry doorToughened or laminated10–12mmAS 1288 / AS 2208
Frameless shower screenToughened monolithic10mmAS 1288 / AS 2208
Acoustic partitionDouble glazed unit6-12-6AS 1288 / AS 4666
Balustrade infillToughened laminated10+10mmAS 1288 / AS 1178

Indicative only — every job is selected on its own measurements and location. Read how AS 1288 is applied.

Ask us
Questions

Asked often.

Do you give prices over the phone?
No — and it is not a sales tactic. Glass is cut to the opening, and the make-up that is legal for that location (AS 1288) changes with the application, the panel size and what is above and below it. A number given without seeing the opening is a number that will not hold. Measure and quote is free across Melbourne metro; the quote names the glass, the thickness and what is included.
Are you licensed and insured?
Anderson Glass & Glazing Pty Ltd is a registered company (ACN 169 467 216, ABN 24 202 344 169) working across Melbourne metro. Current public liability and workers compensation details are provided with any quote or site induction pack.
What areas do you cover?
Melbourne metro, with same-week visits across the south east, inner east, north and bayside. Gippsland work is scheduled in blocks. If you are further out, ask — larger jobs travel.
How long does a job take?
Shower screens and mirrors are typically one to two weeks from measure, because toughened glass is made to your dimensions. Replacement glass can be same-day or next-day for common sizes. Installation itself is usually a few hours. Lead times are confirmed at quote, not published, because they move with supplier load.
Do you supply and install, or install only?
Both supply and install. That is the point: one party accountable for the measurement, the glass, the hardware and the finished result.
What should I send with an enquiry?
A photo of the opening, a rough width and height if you can, the suburb, and whether it is a new build or a replacement. A photo tells us more than a paragraph, and rough dimensions let us tell you what is possible before a site visit.
