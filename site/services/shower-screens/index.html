Shower screens

Frameless and semi-frameless screens, measured and installed.

A frameless shower screen is 10mm toughened safety glass, cut and drilled to your opening and fixed with hinges and brackets rather than a frame. Anderson Glass measures the actual opening — walls are never square — fabricates to those millimetres, and installs with the door swing and screen heights set before the glass is tempered.

What it involves

Shower screens fail at the measurements, not the glass. Out-of-square walls, a fall in the floor, a tile edge that sits proud — each one millimetres, and each one enough to make a pre-made screen leak or a door bind.

Typical scope

  • Frameless 10mm screens and doors
  • Semi-frameless and framed screens
  • Pivot, hinged and sliding configurations
  • Return panels and fixed panels
  • Mirrors and bathroom splashbacks
  • Replacement glass for existing screens

Questions

Asked about
shower screens.

Do you measure before or after tiling?
After. Screen dimensions need the finished tile face and floor fall, so measuring happens once tiling and waterproofing are complete.
Can you replace just the glass?
Often — if the hinges and brackets are reusable and the opening has not moved. Send a photo of the hardware and the screen and we will tell you.
How long between measure and install?
Typically one to two weeks, because toughened glass has to be cut, drilled and heat-treated to your exact dimensions. Confirm the date at quote.
