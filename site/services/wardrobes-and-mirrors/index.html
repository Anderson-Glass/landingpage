Wardrobes & mirrors

Mirror wardrobe doors, splashbacks and wall mirrors.

Anderson Glass supplies and installs mirror wardrobe doors, glass splashbacks and wall mirrors — measured to the opening, cut with polished or bevelled edges, and fixed with hardware rated for the door weight. Sliding mirror doors are the usual choice where swing space is tight.

ScopeWhat this covers

What it involves.

Mirror is unforgiving: it shows every millimetre of an out-of-square wall and every mark left on the back of the glass. The installation matters as much as the mirror itself.

Typical scope

  • Sliding mirror wardrobe doors
  • Hinged mirror doors
  • Glass splashbacks
  • Gym and wall mirrors
  • Replacement mirror for existing tracks
  • Safety-backed mirror where required

NotesWardrobes & mirrors
Can you replace mirror only, keeping the tracks?
Often yes, if the track is sound and the door size is standard. Photograph the track and the top roller and we can advise.
Do mirrors need safety backing?
Where they could be struck or where Council or a spec calls for it. In gyms and bathrooms we default to safety-backed.
