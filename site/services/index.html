How we work

Every line above runs on the same three rules. Measure the opening as it is now, not as the drawing says it will be. Name the glass make-up and the standard it is selected under, in writing. Install it with the person who measured it on site.

It sounds obvious. It is also the difference between a screen that closes cleanly in five years and one that does not close cleanly in five weeks.
