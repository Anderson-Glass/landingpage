Office partitions

Framed, frameless and demountable glass partition systems.

Office partition glazing turns floor area into rooms without losing light. Anderson Glass installs framed aluminium, frameless and demountable glass partition systems for Melbourne offices and warehouses — measured to the tenancy, with doors, manifestation and acoustic detailing resolved before fabrication.

What it involves

A partition is a small building inside a building. Head channels have to land on something solid, floors are never as level as the drawings say, and the acoustic result depends on the detail at the frame, not the glass alone.

Typical scope

  • Framed aluminium partition systems
  • Frameless butt-jointed glass walls
  • Demountable and relocatable partitions
  • Glass and timber doors, patch fittings, closers
  • Manifestation and privacy film to AS 1428 sight lines
  • Acoustic and double-glazed options

Questions

Asked about
office partitions.

Can partitions be added while we keep working?
Usually yes. Partition glazing is staged in sections, and the noisiest work — cutting, drilling, silicone — is scheduled before hours or in a defined window.
Do I need consent or a permit?
That depends on the building and whether the work touches base build services, fire systems or egress. We will tell you when a partition run is likely to need building approval before we quote it.
Can I reuse partitions when I move?
If they were built demountable. A frameless system is generally fixed to the tenancy — worth deciding at the start.
