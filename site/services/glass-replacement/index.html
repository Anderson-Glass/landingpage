Glass replacement

Broken windows, doors and shopfronts — make safe and replace.

Glass replacement covers broken or cracked windows, doors, shopfronts and mirrors. Anderson Glass makes the opening safe first, measures the frame as it actually is, and replaces the pane in the correct make-up and thickness for that location under AS 1288 — not simply the thickness the old pane happened to be.

ScopeWhat this covers

What it involves.

A broken pane is rarely just a broken pane. The glass broke for a reason — impact, movement, or a make-up that was never right for the location — and replacing like-for-like can mean replacing it again.

Typical scope

  • Emergency make-safe and board-up
  • Window and door glass replacement
  • Shopfront and entry glass
  • Mirror and splashback replacement
  • Upgrade to safety glass where required
  • Insurance documentation and photos

NotesGlass replacement
Can you make it safe today?
Most days, yes — board-up or removal of loose glass can usually be arranged the same day across Melbourne metro. Call rather than email for anything unsafe.
Will insurance cover it?
Depends on your policy. We photograph the damage, note the make-up replaced and provide an invoice you can submit.
The old glass was thinner — do I have to upgrade?
If the location now requires safety glass, yes. A glazier replacing glass in a regulated location is bound by AS 1288 regardless of what was there before.
