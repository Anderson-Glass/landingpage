# Review request kit

The thing that moves local search is not more suburb names on the website — it's reviews on your
Google profile. Competitors ranking for "glazier Narre Warren" have 7–116 reviews. You have none,
because nobody has asked yet.

Ask at the moment the job is finished. That's when goodwill is highest and the phone is already in
your hand.

## Rules

- **Ask in person, then send the link.** "If you were happy with it, a Google review helps me a lot —
  I'll text you the link." One ask, no follow-up guilt trip.
- **Never offer anything in exchange.** Discounts, gifts or entries into a draw for a review breach
  Google's policy and can wipe the reviews.
- **Never write it for them, and never post from their phone.** Both are detectable and both are
  fraud.
- **Ask everyone**, including the small jobs. A broken window replacement with a good review is worth
  as much as a fitout.

## The link

Your review link (get the exact one from your Business Profile: "Ask for reviews" → copy link):

```
https://g.page/r/<YOUR-PLACE-ID>/review
```

Save it as a phone shortcut or a QR code on the back of your quote pad. Typing it out on site is
friction people won't clear.

## SMS — the one that works

> Hi {first name}, Ben from Anderson Glass. Thanks for the job today — if you were happy with it, a
> quick Google review helps me a lot as a small business: {link}
> No worries at all if not. Cheers.

Short, no pressure, no marketing language. Send within an hour of leaving.

## Email — for commercial clients

**Subject:** Thanks — and a small ask

> Hi {name},
>
> Thanks for having us out to {site or job}. The {what you installed} is done and should give you no
> trouble — if anything moves or binds in the first year, call me directly on 0414 441 192.
>
> One ask: if the job went well, a short Google review genuinely helps a small glazing business get
> found by the next customer. It takes about a minute: {link}
>
> Either way, thanks for the work.
>
> Ben
> Anderson Glass & Glazing

## Reply to every review

Google looks at whether the business engages. One line is enough:

- **5 star:** "Thanks {name} — glad the screens went in cleanly. Call if anything needs adjusting."
- **3 star or less:** reply with the fix, not a defence: "That's not the standard I want on a job —
  give me a call on 0414 441 192 and I'll sort it."

## Where reviews pay twice

Your Google reviews feed:
- the map pack and "near me" results
- the knowledge panel people see before they call
- the directories that currently outrank you (hipages, Word of Mouth) — a business with visible
  reviews gets listed and ranked there too

## The number to watch

Ask for one review per job for four weeks and you'll have more than most glaziers in Casey. The
website's daily enquiry line tells you whether it's working.
