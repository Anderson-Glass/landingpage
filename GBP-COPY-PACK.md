## Business description (748 characters, under the 750 limit)

Anderson Glass & Glazing supplies and installs glass across Melbourne's south east and beyond —
commercial glazing, office partitions, frameless shower screens, replacement glass, mirrors and
wardrobe doors. The glazier who quotes your job is the one who measures it and the one who installs it,
so the glass is cut to your actual opening rather than to a drawing. We work to AS 1288 for glass
selection, and provide glazing certificates where a job requires one. Shopfronts, fitouts, bathrooms
and broken windows — measured, made and installed with one person accountable from quote to finished
silicone. Free measure and quote across Melbourne metro. Call 0414 441 192.
